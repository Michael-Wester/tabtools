> TabTools

Short description:

> Clear your tab list quickly with site suggestions, inactive tab cleanup, duplicate removal, sorting, and undo.

Description:

> Close tabs by entering a keyword or domain, or use automatic suggestions based on the sites you have open. TabTools also provides quick actions to close inactive tabs, remove duplicates, sort tabs by site frequency, and undo your most recent cleanup.

> - Site suggestions based on open-tab counts
> - Close inactive tabs using a configurable time threshold

> - Sort tabs by site frequency
> - Undo recently closed tabs
