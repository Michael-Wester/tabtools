# What's new in TabTools 5.0.0

English text for Firefox Add-ons' release-notes field and the GitHub release.
Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons have no such field. The technical notes are in
[`docs/releases/5.0.0.md`](../../docs/releases/5.0.0.md). Translations follow with
the rest of the 5.0.0 translations.

## Text

A new popup, and safer cleanup.

- One list instead of the grid: Inactive, Close duplicates, then every site with open tabs, busiest first, with a mark for each tab. A long list scrolls; nothing is left out.
- Type a keyword or a site to see the matching tabs before anything closes. Press Enter to close the ones listed, or close them one at a time. A site such as google.com now includes its subdomains.
- Inactive opens a list to review, with the time since each tab was last used. Nothing closes until you press Close. Choose the time from 30 minutes to 1 week.
- Pinned tabs now stay open when you close a site's tabs. Turn off "Keep pinned tabs open" in Settings to close them too.
- Close duplicates only treats tabs as copies when their whole addresses match, so two different conversations in a mail app are no longer duplicates. A pinned copy, a copy playing sound and the copy you are looking at always stay.
- Sort tabs leaves pinned tabs and tab groups where they are.
- A tab you were using a moment ago is no longer listed as inactive.
- Undo is offered for a few seconds after a close, in a result bar at the bottom of the popup.
- New in Settings: a System theme that follows your browser, six accent colours, and a Reset for the count of tabs closed. The minimum-tabs setting is gone: every site is listed.

Your settings carry over. A saved inactivity time shorter than 30 minutes becomes 30 minutes.
