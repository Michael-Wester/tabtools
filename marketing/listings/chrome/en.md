## Full description

Close tabs by site, sort your tab bar, and clear duplicates with a free, open-source Chrome extension.

⚡ Close a site’s tabs together
Open TabTools and click a site under Suggestions, or right-click within a web page and choose Close site tabs. This closes matching tabs across your regular windows, including the current tab and pinned tabs.

🗂️ Sort tabs by site
Bring tabs from the same site together in your current window, with the sites you have open most placed first.

🧹 Remove duplicate tabs
Close extra copies of the same page in one click. Pinned tabs are left in place.

🔎 Search by keyword or domain
Type a word to match tab titles and URLs, or enter an exact domain such as youtube.com. Then choose Close.

⏳ Clear inactive tabs
Choose an inactivity threshold in Settings, then click Inactive to clear eligible tabs. Active, pinned, and audible tabs stay open.

🔒 The TabTools extension processes tab information on your device. Settings and cleanup statistics stay in your browser’s local storage. The extension has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or external database.
