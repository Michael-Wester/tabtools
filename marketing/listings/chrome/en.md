TabTools - Close & Sort Tabs by Site

## Full description

Close tabs by site, sort your tab bar, and clear duplicates with a free, open-source Chrome extension.

⚡ Close a site’s tabs together
Open TabTools and click a site under Suggestions, or right-click within a web page and choose Close site tabs. This closes that site’s tabs across your regular windows, including the one you are on. Pinned tabs stay open unless you turn that off in Settings.

🗂️ Sort tabs by site
Bring tabs from the same site together in your current window, with the sites you have open most placed first. Pinned tabs and tab groups stay where they are.

🧹 Remove duplicate tabs
Close extra copies of the same page in one click. Two tabs are copies only when their whole addresses match. A pinned copy, a copy playing sound and the copy you are looking at always stay.

🔎 Search by keyword or domain
Type a word to list the tabs whose titles or addresses contain it, or a site such as youtube.com to list that site and its subdomains. Check the list, then press Enter or choose Close.

⏳ Clear inactive tabs
Click Inactive to see the tabs you have not used for a while, with the time since each was last used. Choose how long counts as inactive, from 30 minutes to 1 week, then close the ones listed. Pinned, playing and current tabs stay open.

🔒 The TabTools extension processes tab information on your device. Settings and cleanup statistics stay in your browser’s local storage. The extension has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or external database.
